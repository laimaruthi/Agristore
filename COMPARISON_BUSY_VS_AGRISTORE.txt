WHO SHOULD USE AGRISTORE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Small to medium agriculture store
- Owner-operated (1-2 people)
- Rural area with poor internet
- Need simple billing & customer tracking
- Want one-time cost, no yearly fees
- Don't need complex GST filing (CA handles it)


WHO SHOULD USE BUSY?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Large business with multiple employees
- Need to file GST returns directly from software
- Multiple branches/locations
- Need advanced accounting (P&L, Balance Sheet)
- Have accountant using the software daily

SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
